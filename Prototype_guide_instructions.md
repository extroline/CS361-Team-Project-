## How to Use the Prototype

### Patron Portal Sections
1. **Register & Waiver:** Click **Register**, enter a valid test library card number, and sign the electronic liability waiver to create a new patron account.
2. **Dashboard:** View your borrowed tools (out of the 5-tool limit), active reservations, overdue banners, and outstanding fees.
3. **Browse Tools:** Search by keyword, filter by category, availability, or kits only, and click **View details** to reserve an available tool (3-day hold) or join the waitlist if all units are out.
4. **My Loans:** View active loans, request a one-time renewal (blocked if the tool is overdue or has a waitlist), and view past borrowing history.
5. **Reservations:** Manage your 3-day pickup holds and waitlist positions (cancel or leave waitlist).
6. **Fees:** Review late charges (1-day grace period, $1/day standard or $3/day high-demand, capped at $20 or replacement value), pay fees online, and view payment receipts.

### Staff / Admin Portal Sections
1. **Checkout:** Look up a patron, run the 5-part eligibility check (including checking the **Photo ID shows patron is 18 or older** box), and scan/select an available tool barcode to check it out for up to 14 days.
2. **Return:** Scan/select a checked-out tool (e.g., `BC-ES-001`), inspect its condition and kit components, complete the return, and optionally flag it **Out of Service**. Returning a waitlisted tool automatically promotes the next patron in line to a 3-day pickup hold.
3. **Renewals:** Approve or deny pending patron renewal requests (with automatic waitlist checks).
4. **Inventory:** Expand tool types to view individual units/barcodes, edit tool info, flag/clear maintenance status, retire non-checked-out tools, or add new tools using the form on the right.
5. **Reservations:** View all active holds and waitlists, cancel entries, or create reservations on a patron's behalf.
6. **Unreturned:** View all overdue loans and open any patron's account to record in-person fee payments (Cash, Card, or Check).
7. **Reports:** Switch between the four live reports: **Currently checked out**, **Overdue**, **In repair**, and **Most borrowed**.
